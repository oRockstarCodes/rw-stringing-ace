**Term:** Fall 2026 · **Status:** in progress

## Summary

The software development cycle (requirements, implementation, testing, debugging) through object-oriented programming in Java: classes and objects, design, JUnit testing, inheritance, polymorphism, and exceptions.

## Textbooks

- Sierra & Bates, *Head First Java*, 2nd ed. (required)
- Lewis & Loftus, *Java Software Solutions*, 4th ed. (reference; chapter numbers below refer to this)
