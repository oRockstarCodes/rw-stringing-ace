**Term:** Fall 2026 · **Status:** in progress

## Summary

Continues circuit analysis from ELE 202: op amps, second-order transients, frequency response and Bode plots, the Laplace transform, magnetically coupled circuits, three-phase power, and Fourier series. Labs use PSpice and hands-on experiments.

## Textbooks

- Alexander & Sadiku, *Fundamentals of Electric Circuits*, 6th ed.
