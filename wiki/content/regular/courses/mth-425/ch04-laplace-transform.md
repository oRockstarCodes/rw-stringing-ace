## Overview

The definition of the Laplace transform, the start of chapter 4.

## Notes

The Laplace transform is a type of integral. $f(t)$ must converge, so that limit has to exist.

$$
\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\} = \int_0^{\infty} e^{-st} f(t)\,dt = F(s)
$$

## Key formulas
