## Overview

Where electromagnetic waves come from, what "polarized" means, and how the EM spectrum is laid out.

## Notes

**Linearly polarized waves** have electric and magnetic fields restricted to being parallel to a pair of perpendicular axes.

**Accelerated charges** are the source of electromagnetic waves. This is how electronic devices create EM waves.

The **electromagnetic spectrum** runs from waves with long wavelengths and low frequencies to waves with short wavelengths and high frequencies.
