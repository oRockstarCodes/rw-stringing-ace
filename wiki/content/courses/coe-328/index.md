**Term:** Fall 2026 · **Status:** in progress

## Summary

Digital logic from the ground up: Boolean algebra, implementation technology (transistors to CPLDs and FPGAs), logic optimization, arithmetic circuits, combinational and sequential design, and an introduction to datapaths and CPUs. Labs use VHDL and CAD tools on FPGA boards.

## Textbooks

- Brown & Vranesic, *Fundamentals of Digital Logic with VHDL Design*, 3rd ed. (chapter numbers below)
- Hayes, *Introduction to Digital Logic Design*
