**Term:** Fall 2026 · **Status:** in progress

## Summary

The physics behind semiconductor devices: a quick electrostatics and quantum review, band theory, carrier statistics and doping, the p-n junction, optoelectronic devices, and the MOSFET.

## Textbooks

- Serway & Jewett, *Physics for Scientists and Engineers*, 10th ed. (**SJ**): early weeks
- Neamen, *Semiconductor Physics and Devices*, 4th ed. (**N**): most of the course
