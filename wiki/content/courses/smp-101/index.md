## Summary

DC circuit analysis from the ground up: the basic laws, simplifying networks, and the theorems that make bigger circuits manageable.

## Key takeaways

1. Ohm's law, KCL, and KVL are enough to solve any linear DC circuit. Everything else is a shortcut.
2. Simplify before you solve: combine series and parallel parts, use dividers.
3. For a load connected to a complicated network, replace the network with its Thévenin equivalent.
